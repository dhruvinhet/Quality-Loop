# T18 — Evidence correctness fixes from re-audit

Implemented R-01 refill, R-04 hint disclosure, R-05 decision availability, and conservative candidate counts. Matching rules, evaluation inputs, manifest, scopes, search caps, and the shared 15-second loading budget are unchanged. The hidden `eval/test/` split was not opened or modified.

## Behavior and files changed

- `src/resolvers/evidence.js`: sort the search candidates for each signature class, then re-read in batches of at most five until 25 matches have been verified or the candidates are exhausted. Changed captures and 403/404 responses no longer prevent an older valid candidate from being checked. Each batch is bounded by the number of remaining display slots. Completed reads survive a budget failure elsewhere in their batch. The other-related queries retain their existing per-query behavior.
- `src/resolvers/evidence.js`: maintain `notShown.strong` and `notShown.relatedRevision` from candidates obtained in the invocation's `asUser()` search snapshot whose re-read has not completed. Completed denials and changed captures do not inflate these numbers. No Jira total or storage count is used. Set `uncheckedCandidates` whenever either signature class has unchecked candidates, including a budget failure during search pagination. Unchecked candidates stay out of historical-pair loading.
- `src/frontend/EvidenceResults.jsx`: use definitive no-match wording only when `matchesIncomplete`, `incomplete`, and `uncheckedCandidates` are all false. Otherwise show **No exact signature match found in the completed checks.** Display **{n} more candidates not checked (most recent 25 verified matches shown).** for each capped class. This describes candidates from the search snapshot without asserting their current visibility or validity.
- `src/resolvers/index.js`: flag hint results `incomplete` and `truncated` when any capped search page reports `isLast: false`, a continuation token, or more rows than the cap. Keep time-budget exhaustion distinct with `budgetExceeded`; do not follow hint continuation tokens.
- `src/frontend/HintResults.jsx`: display **The hint search was limited to 50 records; more may exist.** for truncation. Replace the categorical recurrence statement with **These records do not meet the matching rules, but may still be worth a manual review.** A budget notice appears when the hint budget actually expires.
- `src/resolvers/decisionStore.js`: allow the essential direct pair-state/version read to use the caller's budget. Reading state does not load aggregate counts or derive legacy confirmation history.
- `src/resolvers/decisions.js`: load and retain essential state/version for all displayed cards before optional historical-pair discovery, history, names, and aggregate counts. Start optional fields as unavailable and publish them only after their work completes. A timeout preserves already loaded state/version, so completed cards remain actionable; an unfinished essential read never becomes an invented empty state. Count permissions, direct state verification, distinct-NC deduplication, and legacy-history handling remain in place.
- `src/frontend/MatchView.jsx`: optional decision-history/count incompleteness no longer disables every card. Refreshing still disables decisions.
- `src/frontend/DecisionDetails.jsx`: require the card's completed pair state before enabling decisions. Show **Count unavailable — refresh to try again** when optional count loading did not complete. Do not show **No decisions recorded.** when history itself is incomplete.
- `docs/tasks/T18.md`: document behavior, verification, limitations, and owner checks.

## Owner click/check walkthrough

1. Open an editable saved NC with a detected date, open **QualityLoop** under the issue's apps menu, and click **Refresh evidence and decisions**. Check that strong and related-revision cards remain separate, with at most 25 verified cards in each class.
2. Use an NC with more than 25 candidates in either class. Check **{n} more candidates not checked (most recent 25 verified matches shown).** The number describes unchecked search candidates, not currently visible or valid matches. If no strong card is displayed while any signature candidates remain unchecked, verify **No exact signature match found in the completed checks.**, with no definitive no-CAPA sentence.
3. Expand **Not matched — check these**. For a hint search with a continuation, verify **The hint search was limited to 50 records; more may exist.** Verify **These records do not meet the matching rules, but may still be worth a manual review.** and that hints have no decision buttons.
4. On a card whose pair state loaded but whose count timed out, check **Count unavailable — refresh to try again**. **Engineer-confirmed recurrence** and **Not a recurrence** must remain enabled when the NC is editable and refresh has finished; concern eligibility still follows its existing rules. Click **Engineer-confirmed recurrence**, verify the decision form opens, and click **Cancel** to avoid a write during this check.
5. On a card whose essential pair state did not load, verify its decision buttons are disabled even if other cards are actionable. Click **Refresh evidence and decisions** to retry; decisions remain disabled during refresh.
6. Refill races and precise timeout timing are not reliably reproducible by clicking alone. `npm test` deterministically covers both signature classes with 25 newer failed revalidations, permission changes, count/history expiry, and essential-state expiry. Use those regressions to verify these conditions when live Jira does not exhibit them.
