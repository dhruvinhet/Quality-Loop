## Files changed and why

- `src/frontend/HintResults.jsx`: hints start expanded when nonempty. An effect
  also expands them when hints arrive after a load/empty result. Manual collapse
  survives a refresh while hints remain nonempty; an empty-to-nonempty transition
  reopens the section. Zero hints hide the hint section and toggle. Error,
  truncation and time-budget notices remain visible independently so an empty
  partial/error result is not silently presented as a complete search.
  An information SectionMessage prominently states that these are not matches,
  including while manually collapsed. The revised copy follows the review:

  > Up to 3 hints, from limited searches (at most 50 candidates each): near-identical part numbers, same part with no closed date, and older precedents. These are not matches.

  Both numbers come from `HINT_LIMIT` / `HINT_CANDIDATE_LIMIT`. The truncation
  notice likewise describes 50 candidates **per search**, not 50 records total
  or “recently closed” records. Manual-review and browse-access wording remains.
- `src/frontend/projectPage.jsx`: adds **How to try this**, three compact numbered
  steps covering confirmed sample creation, readiness/opening the NC/panel/Save,
  and evidence review plus a reasoned engineer decision. Once readiness returns,
  step two links to that authorised `newNcKey`. No admin page or extra backend
  call is needed. `SamplePage` is exported for tests; its existing native mount,
  resolver calls and write-confirmation flow are unchanged.
- `src/frontend/EvidenceResults.jsx`: strong and related-revision cards append
  `(matched as <partNorm>)` immediately after a differing original part string.
  Clean originals and other-related cards do not get that annotation. The
  normalised value comes from the existing matcher, which re-normalises originals
  in memory. Original text and classification remain unchanged. Fixes day/days,
  candidate/candidates, verified match/matches and other-related record/records.

- `src/frontend/adminPage.jsx`: fixes singular/plural for found, checked, unresolved,

- `src/lib/plural.js`: shared noun selection for exact numeric one versus other

- `src/frontend/__tests__/hintResults.test.js`: initial and delayed expansion,

## Count and search audit

Reviewed dynamic count text throughout the frontend and resolver/lib-produced UI
messages. All dynamic counts that can equal one now use singular nouns. Existing
older-precedent hints always exceed the fixed 365-day window, so their elapsed-day
count is at least 366 and correctly remains plural. Fixed limits of 3/5/20/25/50/
200 and 30 seconds remain plural. No search limits, matching rules, hint selection,
permissions, totals or storage changed.

The three hint queries are unchanged: closure-window candidates without a part
filter; same-part candidates without a date filter (checked for missing closure);
and same-part candidates before the window. Each fetches at most 50 candidates;
fresh authorised records are classified before at most three hints are shown.

T24's legacy-index limitation remains: stored trailing-space `qlPartBase` values
can still require an owner re-save before JQL finds them. A display annotation
does not migrate or broaden the search.

## Exactly what to click and check live

1. Open a Jira project → **QualityLoop**. Follow **How to try this** without
   visiting admin. If no sample exists, click **Create sample scenario**, review
   and confirm its listed writes. Once ready (or after **Check sample readiness**),
   confirm step two links to the **DEMO new NC**. Open it, open QualityLoop from
   the apps icon, and click **Save**.
2. On an NC with a known typo/older/legacy-undated hint, refresh evidence. Hints
   must be visible without clicking, including when they arrive after loading.
   Confirm the information banner says **These are not matches** and accurately
   describes three hints / 50 candidates per search. Collapse manually: the
   disclaimer stays visible. An NC with no hints has no hint toggle/section.
   Search errors/incomplete notices should still be visible when applicable.
3. On test records with CAPA original `T24-6604 -A` and NC original `T24-6604- A`,
   same failure mode and a CAPA closed one day before detection, Save and refresh
   after indexing. Confirm **T24-6604 -A (matched as T24-6604-A)** and **1 day after
   closure**. A two-day gap says **2 days**. A different revision remains related
   and shows its own normalised part, never an exact-match label.
4. Confirm an engineer decision with a reason: after the count refreshes, one
   confirmed NC uses singular wording. Check a single other-related record label
   when available. Candidate/omitted-match singular cases are covered by unit
   tests and do not require creating hundreds of live issues.
5. On admin Data health, check displayed counts and, if one orphan is available,
   open its removal confirmation and **Cancel** to inspect singular wording.
   Removal result wording is covered by tests; no live deletion is needed for QA.
6. Recheck T25b: fast decision typing/paste stays complete; correcting a capture
   error does not remove it on blur, and the first Save works; URL-specific
   messages and styled insecure-link warnings remain. Also retain T23's date/type
   protections. Review on GitHub and test live before the next prompt.
